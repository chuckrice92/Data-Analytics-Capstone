# Restaurant Revenue Analysis

## Project Overview

Analyzed the **Restaurant Revenue Prediction Dataset** from Kaggle to identify the factors most strongly associated with restaurant revenue and develop data-driven business recommendations.

**Tools:** Excel | Tableau

**Skills:** Data Cleaning | Exploratory Data Analysis | PivotTables | Correlation Analysis | Data Visualization | Business Insights

## Key Findings

* **Average meal price** had the strongest relationship with revenue.
* **Seating capacity** was the second strongest revenue-related factor.
* **Marketing budget** had a stronger relationship with revenue than social media following, challenging the original hypothesis.
* Customer service-related measures showed relatively weak relationships with revenue.
* Downtown locations and Japanese cuisine were associated with stronger revenue performance in the dataset.

## Business Recommendations

The analysis suggests that restaurants should focus on:

* Optimizing pricing based on customer value and market demand.
* Maximizing seating capacity while maintaining strong occupancy.
* Investing in broader marketing strategies rather than focusing solely on social media growth.
* Considering location and cuisine positioning when evaluating revenue opportunities.
